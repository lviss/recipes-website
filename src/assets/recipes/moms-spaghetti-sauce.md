# Mom's spaghetti sauce

## Ingredients

* 3lb ground beef
* 2lb sausage
* 2 heaping soup spoons of garlic
* 3 chopped onions
* 1 teaspoon rosemary
* 2 teaspoon oregano
* 1 teaspoon cumin
* 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
* 1 teaspoon basil
* 4 cans of 15 oz tomato sauce

## Directions

Saute until very brown, pretty much burnt, on 8

Turn down to 5
Add two heaping soup spoons of garlic
3 chopped onions

Drain fat

Add teaspoon rosemary, 2 teaspoon oregano, teaspoon cumin, Worcestershire 1/4 cup, 1 teaspoon basil

Stir

Add 4 cans of 15 oz tomato sauce

Cook all day long
